Flying In

Chicago O'Hare (ORD) and Midway (MDW) both serve the city. From ORD, the Blue Line CTA runs directly downtown in about 45 minutes. Rideshare is 30–40 minutes depending on traffic.

Hotel Block

We've reserved a room block at the Belden-Stratford, a historic landmark steps from Lincoln Park. Use code SMMOLLY26 for our rate. Block expires September 1, 2026.

Getting Around

The conservatory and Nic + Junior's are both in Lincoln Park, less than 10 minutes apart. We'll arrange a shuttle between venues for guests. Details to follow closer to the date.
